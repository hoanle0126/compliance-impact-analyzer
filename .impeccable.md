# ImpactLens Design System & Aesthetic Brief

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience:** Enterprise compliance officers, customs brokers, corporate legal counsel, and trade analysts.
- **Context:** Reviewing complex statutory amendments (Vietnamese Ministry of Finance & Customs circulars) to determine enterprise liability, operational disruptions, and mandatory filings.
- **Job to be Done:** Rapidly digest what changed in the law, verify why a specific client organization is affected, inspect official evidence citations, and assign operational action items with human sign-off.
- **Emotional Goal:** Authoritative trust, extreme legal clarity, zero alert fatigue, and calm executive decision-making.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone:** Authoritative, razor-sharp, objective, and unimpressed by fluff.
- **3-Word Descriptor:** *Judicious, Precise, Architectural.*
- **Anti-References:** Cheap AI cyber dashboards, purple/cyan neon gradients, cards nested inside cards, emoji clutter, and playful SaaS landing pages.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Direction:** **High-End Legal Editorial Instrument (Refined Clean Light)**.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: Fine parchment/cream off-white (`#F8FAFC` / `#F9F9FB`).
  - Elevated surfaces: Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with razor-thin 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
  - Subtle contrasts: Slate-50 background for secondary utility panels.
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: Crisp high-contrast Swiss-style sans (`Inter`, `system-ui`) with tight tracking (`tracking-tight`) and deliberate optical hierarchy.
  - Legal Articles & Quotes: High-legibility editorial serif/sans with generous leading (`leading-relaxed`), capped at 68ch line-length.
  - Data & Citations: Crisp tabular monospace (`font-mono`) for document numbers, HS codes, dates, and evidence IDs (`[EV-001]`).
- **Color Discipline:**
  - Primary text: Deep ink charcoal (`#0F172A`).
  - Secondary text: Muted slate (`#475569`), Caption/Meta: (`#64748B`).
  - Brand Accent: Deep legal navy (`#1E3A8A` / `#0F2942`) and sharp royal ink (`#1D4ED8`).
  - Status Indicators:
    - Applies / High Severity: Deep crimson tint (`bg-rose-50 text-rose-800 border-rose-200`).
    - Potentially Applies / Review: Warm ochre tint (`bg-amber-50 text-amber-800 border-amber-200`).
    - Compliant / Approved: Forest green tint (`bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-800 border-emerald-200`).
    - Informational / Meta: Subtle slate tint (`bg-slate-100 text-slate-700 border-slate-200`).

### Absolute Bans & Anti-Slop Rules
1. **NO Gradient Text:** No `bg-clip-text text-transparent bg-gradient-to-r`.
2. **NO Side-Stripe Accent Borders:** No `border-l-4` or `border-l-2` colored stripes.
3. **NO Cards-in-Cards Monoculture:** Do not wrap every tiny label or metric in a rounded bordered box. Use whitespace, typography hierarchy, and hairline dividers instead.
4. **NO Neon AI Glow / Cyan-on-Dark:** All colors are calibrated for high-contrast light background reading.
5. **NO Emojis as System Icons:** Use sleek, lightweight Lucide vector icons (`w-4 h-4`) with semantic color.
